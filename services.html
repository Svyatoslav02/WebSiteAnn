Approach

From idea to outcome I take care of every step!

Concept

This is the most open and creative stage — sometimes the most challenging. Tell me what inspires you, what you like (or don’t), and the feelings you want your brand or project to convey. Together, we’ll shape a unique concept step by step.

What’s included:

  • • Idea generation
  • • Moodboard creation
  • • Client communication and inspiration gathering
  • • Defining brand personality and target audience
  • • Initial sketches

Plan, Quote & Design

Every project starts with a clear plan. Once the concept and pricing are agreed upon, I’ll begin working on the design with focus and clear direction. A 50% upfront payment is required to start.

What’s included:

  • • Project planning and key milestones
  • • Budget estimation and cost agreement
  • • Research and trend analysis
  • • Development of visual elements and style
  • • Typography and color palette selection

Presentation

Depending on the project, I will prepare several design options. A thorough presentation will help you choose the best one. After your approval, I’ll prepare all necessary files and assets for smooth delivery.

What’s included:

  • • Preparing multiple design options
  • • Mockups and real-life application examples
  • • Client discussion and feedback collection
  • • Revisions and refinements

Approval & Delivery

Post-production emerges as the pivotal phase in creative projects, wherein raw content undergoes a transformation into its refined, polished state where editing, sound design, visual effects, and essential adjustments to breathe life into the project.

What’s included:

  • • Preparation of final files (PDF, AI, JPG, PSD, SVG, TIFF)
  • • Organizing files for easy access
  • • Delivery via secure download link
  • • Transfer of usage rights after full payment

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Find answers to common questions clients ask before working with me.

Project timelines vary based on the scope and complexity:
Smaller projects — like a logo, banner, or simple landing page — typically take 1–2 weeks
Medium to large projects — such as a full website or complete branding package — take around 3–5 weeks or more Once I learn more about your needs, I’ll provide a tailored timeline with clear milestones.

Prices are based on project size, deliverables, and technical needs. Here are some starting points for freelance design work in Europe:
Logo design — from €100
Mini branding package (logo, color palette, typography) — from €200–400
Website UI/UX design (up to 5 pages) — from €400–700
Design + Webflow development — from €700–1200
💳 Payment is split into two parts: 50% upfront, and 50% after project completion and approval.

My workflow is collaborative, clear, and structured. Here’s what to expect:
Discovery / Briefing — we define your goals, audience, style, and needs
Concept development — I create initial directions or UI mockups
Feedback & iteration — we refine the design together based on your input
Final design — I deliver the final versions in high-quality formats
(Optional) Webflow development — I turn the designs into a fully responsive, live site
Handoff & support — you get all files, guides, and (if needed) a short video tutorial.

Your input is important and valued at key stages:
You’ll provide direction at the start (briefing)
I’ll update you regularly and request feedback during revisions
You’ll approve final versions before delivery
I aim to make the process smooth and low-stress while keeping you in creative control.

Which languages can we work in? I work in English, Ukrainian, and Polish — feel free to communicate in whichever is most comfortable for you.
